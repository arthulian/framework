Specification Framework

Preserve the integrity of Perception, Reasoning, Self-Regulation, Identity, and Action, under pressure conditions.

Truthfulness Alignment of beliefs, representations, and actions with reality as best understood.
Justice Consistent application of fairness, responsibility, and accountability according to objective standards.
Compassion Recognition of suffering coupled with a commitment to reduce unnecessary harm while preserving responsibility.
Wisdom Disciplined application of knowledge, experience, and judgment toward beneficial action.
Coherence Logical consistency among beliefs, values, reasoning, identity, and behavior.
Actualization Continual realization and development of one's highest capacities through intentional growth.
Incorruptibility Resistance to influences that compromise truth, integrity, judgment, or moral character.
Empirical Confidence Confidence proportional to demonstrated competence, maintained through empirical feedback.
I. Perception

Acquisition and interpretation of information.

  • ◇Governed By
    Truthfulness, Wisdom
  • ◇Requires
    Attention, Observation, Curiosity
  • ◇Uses
    Credibility Calibration, Belief Updating, Stress & Bias Mitigation
  • ◇Pipeline
    Observe → Compare Sources → Evaluate Credibility → Detect Bias → Update Beliefs
  • ◇Produces
    Evidence, Situational Awareness, Updated Beliefs
  • ◇Feeds Into
    Reasoning
II. Reasoning

Evaluation and integration of information to produce justified conclusions.

  • ◇Governed By
    Truthfulness, Wisdom, Coherence, Fairness, Incorruptibility
  • ◇Requires
    Evidence, Updated Beliefs, Context
  • ◇Uses
    Steelmanning, Objectivity & Impartiality, Situational Empathy
  • ◇Pipeline
    Interpret → Analyze → Compare → Evaluate Impartially → Test Assumptions → Integrate Context → Form Conclusions
  • ◇Produces
    Understanding, Decisions, Intentions
  • ◇Feeds Into
    Self-Regulation • Action
III. Self-Regulation

Management of thoughts, emotions, and behavior in service of principled action.

  • ◇Governed By
    Wisdom, Compassion, Actualization
  • ◇Requires
    Self-Awareness, Self-Examination, Reasoning
  • ◇Uses
    Grounded Awareness, Acceptance & Recovery, Calibrated Stress Response
  • ◇Pipeline
    Monitor → Regulate Emotion → Recover → Recalibrate
  • ◇Produces
    Emotional Stability, Resilience, Behavioral Readiness
  • ◇Feeds Into
    Identity • Action
IV. Identity

Integrated and enduring organization of one's beliefs, values, character, and self-concept.

  • ◇Governed By
    Coherence, Compassion, Incorruptibility
  • ◇Requires
    Self-Examination, Experience, Self-Regulation
  • ◇Uses
    Intrinsic Self-Worth, Empirical Confidence, Failure as Feedback
  • ◇Pipeline
    Self-Examine → Integrate Experience → Align Values → Preserve Integrity
  • ◇Produces
    Stable Identity, Principled Character
  • ◇Feeds Into
    Action
V. Action

Intentional execution of decisions within the external world.

  • ◇Governed By
    Truthfulness, Wisdom, Coherence, Actualization
  • ◇Requires
    Decisions, Stable Identity, Self-Regulation
  • ◇Uses
    Strategic Focus, Sustainable Persistence, Flexible Commitment
  • ◇Pipeline
    Plan → Prioritize → Execute → Monitor Outcomes → Adjust Strategy
  • ◇Produces
    Outcomes, Experience, Consequences
  • ◇Feeds Into
    Adaptive Performance
VI. Adaptive Performance

Sustaining and improving effective performance through evidence, self-examination, learning, and adaptation.

  • ◇Governed By
    Truthfulness, Wisdom, Actualization
  • ◇Requires
    Outcomes, Evidence, Self-Examination
  • ◇Uses
    Feedback, Learning, Adaptation, Deliberate Practice
  • ◇Pipeline
    Evaluate Outcomes → Identify Errors → Update Beliefs → Refine Strategies → Reinforce Effective Practices
  • ◇Produces
    Improved Perception, Improved Reasoning, Improved Self-Regulation, Improved Identity, Improved Action
  • ◇Feeds Into
    Perception • Reasoning • Self-Regulation • Identity • Action
